# FirstBank United Way Virtual Pie-Throwing Fundraiser
### Concept overview for committee review

**The idea in one sentence:** Employees across the entire footprint pledge United Way donations to "throw pies" at our four Regional Presidents' avatars — live, on their phones, all campaign long.

## How it works
1. Employees open a link (savable to any phone's home screen — no app store, nothing installed).
2. They pledge a donation — every dollar goes to United Way — and receive pies to throw.
3. They fling the pies at a Regional President's avatar and watch the splat land in real time.
4. Everyone sees the action: a live feed of throws, each president's pie count, and the campaign's running total.
5. A twist that raises more money: employees can also pledge to *remove* pies from a president — protecting their favorite while still donating. Every pie, positive or negative, adds to the United Way total.

## What it is NOT (the compliance picture)
- **It never collects money.** No card numbers, no payments, no account numbers — ever. Donations continue through our existing United Way deposit-ticket process, exactly like Jeans Day.
- **It never shames.** There is no individual donor ranking. Competition lives between the four presidents only. Donor names appear in the fun feed, never with dollar amounts ranked.
- **It never exposes data.** Employees enter only a name and region. No logins, no passwords, no personal information.

## What it ADDS for the committee
A daily pledge report (name, region, amount, date) that makes reconciling the United Way account easier than paper tickets — the committee matches pledges to deposits on its own schedule.

## The competition
- The four Regional Presidents, head to head, all campaign.
- Checkpoint moments (mid-October, November) crown the current "leader" for bragging rights.
- Grand finale at campaign close, December 1. (Optional crowd-pleaser, for discussion: the most-pied president takes one real pie on camera.)

## What we need to launch
1. Committee/HR approval of the concept and president avatar style (a preview will be provided for approval before any president's likeness is used — see the working demo).
2. Guidance on device use (personal phones are the plan; bank phones optional).
3. United Way logo permission (we launch FirstBank-branded either way).

## Cost to the bank
**None.** Built and supported pro bono by Plumbline Studio (Kyle Knight, Roanoke) as a community project supporting United Way.
