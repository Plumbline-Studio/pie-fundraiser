# Q&A Sheet — Anticipated Questions
### For Teresa: answers for the chairperson, HR, and leadership

**Does this app collect or handle money?**
No — by design, never. Employees record pledges only. All actual donations flow through the existing United Way deposit-ticket process at the teller line, unchanged. The app contains no payment processing, card data, or account numbers of any kind.

**How do we know pledges get paid?**
The same honor system we already trust for Jeans Day — with an upgrade: the app produces a daily pledge report (name, region, amount, date) the committee can reconcile against the United Way account. That's easier and more auditable than handwritten tickets alone.

**What employee data does it hold?**
Name and region — that's all. No logins, no passwords, no email addresses, no bank credentials. Data is deleted after the campaign.

**Aren't "negative pies" mean-spirited?**
Every negative pie is a donation too — 100% of all pledges, positive or negative, go to United Way. Negative pies let employees playfully *defend* a president they like, which historically drives totals up (including presidents defending themselves). It's a feature flag: HR can have it switched off at any time, instantly.

**Could this embarrass employees who can only give a little?**
No — there is deliberately no individual donor leaderboard. Nobody's donation amount is ever ranked or compared. Competition exists only between the four Regional Presidents.

**What about the presidents' images?**
Nothing goes live without approval. The demo shows both avatar styles (fun caricature vs. realistic) using a volunteer committee member's photo. HR and each president approve their own avatar before it appears.

**Do employees need to install anything?**
No. It's a normal secure website (HTTPS) that can optionally be saved to a phone's home screen. Nothing from an app store, nothing installed on bank systems.

**What about bank-issued phones and web filtering?**
Personal phones are the primary plan. If the bank wants it available on managed devices, IT can allow the single URL — a one-line change on their side. We'll follow IT's preference.

**Is United Way's brand involved?**
We launch FirstBank-branded only. If United Way grants logo permission, their marks can be added afterward.

**What does this cost the bank?**
Nothing. It's built and supported pro bono by Plumbline Studio (Kyle Knight) as a community contribution to the campaign.

**Who supports it during the campaign?**
Plumbline Studio directly — issues addressed same-day. The committee has an off switch at all times.

**What happens after December 1?**
Final totals and the reconciliation report are handed to the committee, data is purged, and the bank can choose to run it again next year.
